# Homework 1 + Milestone 1: getting your files into your repo

Both are due Sunday at 11:59 PM ET. Both are submitted as your **GitHub repo link**. Use the same **my-first-site** repo you made in Week 1. Your site grows in that one repo all term.

## 1. Put your pages together in one folder
1. Open your **START_site** folder (from today's class).
2. Your **Homework 1 page** (the About page from Lecture 3) becomes your **About** page. Copy it into START_site, along with its **images** folder.
3. Delete the empty **about.html** that came with START_site, then rename your Homework 1 file to **about.html** (all lowercase).
4. Open about.html in VS Code and paste today's menu (the whole `<ul>`) inside its `<nav></nav>`.
5. **Homework 1 is graded on this About page.** Check that it has: at least **5 different semantic tags** (like header, nav, main, section, footer),
   headings in order (**h1, then h2, then h3**), at least one **image with alt text**, and at least one **list** (no list yet? add a `<ul>` of your hobbies).
6. **Milestone 1: every page needs an h1, an h2, and an h3.** All three START pages already have them. Keep them and change the words to your own.
7. Open index.html in your browser and click every menu link on every page.

## 2. Upload everything to your repo
1. On github.com, open your **my-first-site** repo.
2. Click **Add file**, then **Upload files**.
3. Drag in **index.html**, **projects.html**, **about.html**, your **images** folder, and your **wireframe photo** (JPG or PNG):
   a sketch of all 3 pages, plus a short written note on your **colors and fonts**.
4. Click **Commit changes**. Your new index.html replaces last week's. That's fine.
5. Wait a minute, then open your live link (https://YOURNAME.github.io/my-first-site/) and click every menu link.

## 3. Submit
Copy the address of your **repo page** (it starts with https://github.com/) and paste it into **both** assignments on Campuswire.

Stuck? Post in the class feed or come to office hours.
